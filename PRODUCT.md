# ZIVWA website

## Platform
web

## Purpose
Corporate website serving businesses, job seekers, learners and education partners.

## Confirmed requirements
Six pages: Home, About Us, Services, Training & Courses, Current Openings, Contact Us. Continuous course slider and simple FAQ chatbot. Preserve official logo and company facts. Responsive on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile.

## Stack
Dependency-free static HTML/CSS/JavaScript chosen for easy Hostinger upload and maintenance. Build generates all six pages from shared components and JSON data.

## Evidence and open decisions
Original website source is unavailable. Attached instructions and official logo download failed; visible conversation informs implementation. Official logo remains a configurable asset; use a text fallback. No verified active vacancies, fees, placement statistics, testimonials, or partner logos are supplied. Do not invent them. Forms prepare user-reviewed WhatsApp and email drafts; no server-side delivery is claimed.
